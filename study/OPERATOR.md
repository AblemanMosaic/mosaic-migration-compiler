# Operator instructions

Do not start the primary experiment until independent participants, isolated environments, assignment,
budgets, holdouts and analysis are frozen. Keep a custody inventory of source, MMC wheel, task packets,
judge and tool versions. Avoid sharing confidential employer material; use an approved sanitized case.

Give each arm `participant/TASK.md` and staged change packets at the same points. The MMC arm also
receives its selected library and guides; the conventional arm may choose other competent tools.
Both can ask the same discovery questions. Record interventions, clarifications and all revisions.
Do not silently repair only one arm. Charge model-generation and human tuning to actual effort.

The included examples/foundry are public rehearsal material. Write separate holdouts, preferably with
a different evaluator author. Freeze them before any primary run, retain all terminal outcomes, and
publish only consented/appropriately sanitized records. A full delivery includes reference material;
use only the participant packets for isolated participants, not this entire repository.

After independent execution, record partial and failed tasks, aggregate the prespecified outcomes and
have an independent analyst check them. Source integrity and typed receipts are useful custody tools,
not evidence of independence. No primary result is included in Turn 6.
