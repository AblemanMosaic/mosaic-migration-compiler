# Document authority and compatibility

`constitution/` and its pinned FREEZE are the unchanged six-layer program. `model/foundation`,
`model/domain-t2`, `model/operational-t3`, `model/interfaces-t4`, and `model/qualification-t5`
contain exact semantic bodies/commitments. Their realization bindings may be renewed for distribution
metadata, but semantic bodies and corpora do not silently change.

`docs/turn2/PROTOCOL.md`, `docs/turn3/PROTOCOL.md`, `docs/turn4/PROTOCOL.md`,
`docs/turn5/PROTOCOL.md`, and their selected contracts retain their original finite meanings.
`docs/architecture/PROTOCOL-T1.md` is the foundation protocol. A profile is selected explicitly.

`docs/release/` is the preserved Capability Layer T6 operator dossier. Its findings, observed-component
inventory and dependency versions describe 0.1.0a6 and remain historical evidence, not current
v0.1 guidance. Current successor and release guidance is under `docs/turn7/`. Older
installed-version labels in frozen guides refer to their original artifacts. Current distribution
metadata is read with importlib.metadata, not inferred from a historical GUI title.

`study/` is prospective methodology. The historical private `evidence/` directory is intentionally
absent from the public source tree; separately retained records and failures remain owner custody.
Neither study material nor external evidence grants authority, supersedes original expected results,
or proves honest production observations. An executable example demonstrates only its declared fixture scope.
