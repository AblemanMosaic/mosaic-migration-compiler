## Boundaries that must survive changes

Compilation is not eligibility. Signed testimony is not truth or exhaustive discovery.
Eligible evidence is not separately scoped approval. Approval is not proof the effect happened.
An ambiguous return must reconcile; a newer expired observation cannot revive an older positive.
Rollback cannot erase security floors or restore revoked authority. Product controls do not establish
that all development actions were mediated by the product. No test count is independent approval.
